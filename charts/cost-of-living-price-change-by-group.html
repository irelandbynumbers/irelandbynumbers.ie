Change in consumer prices for each main group of goods and services, October 2021 to October 2026.

Price changes by type of spending

Data from Central Statistics Office (CSO), Consumer Price Index (CPM20) (CC BY 4.0).

About this data

Each group's change is calculated from its Consumer Price Index. All items is the change in the overall index, which weights each group by how much households spend on it.

Housing and energy includes rents, mortgage interest, electricity, gas and other home heating fuels.

About this data

Each line is a Consumer Price Index, set to 100 for the average of 2015, so a value of 150 means prices were 50% higher than in 2015. The indices measure the price of the same quantity over time, so they show price changes, not changes in how much households use.

Gas is natural gas supplied through the network. Electricity and gas prices include the effect of government energy credits in the months they were paid, which lowered bills.

Population of each county at each census from 1841 to 2022, as a percentage of its 1841 population. Use the slider to move through the census years.

County populations since 1841

Data from Central Statistics Office (CSO), Population at Each Census (F1001) (CC BY 4.0).

About this data

100% means a county had the same population as in 1841, the last census before the Great Famine. Counties above 200% (Dublin, and more recently some counties around it) are shown in the darkest blue.

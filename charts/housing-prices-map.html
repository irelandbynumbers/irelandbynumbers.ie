About this data

Prices are from stamp duty returns filed with the Revenue Commissioners, for new and existing homes bought by households at market value. They are averages of the prices paid, so they reflect the mix of homes sold as well as price levels, and are not adjusted for inflation.

The average is the total value of sales divided by the number of sales. It is used here rather than the median so that the CSO's figures for city and county areas can be combined. A few very expensive homes can pull the average up. Figures for Dublin add together its four areas, and figures for Cork and Galway add together their city and county areas, to match the county map.

About this data

Completions are counted when a new home is first connected to the electricity network, and are added up from the CSO's quarterly figures for each local authority.

Each year is divided by the county's population at the latest census before or in that year (2011, 2016 or 2022), so the rate is approximate. Figures for Dublin add together its four council areas, and figures for Cork and Galway add together their city and county council areas, to match the county map.

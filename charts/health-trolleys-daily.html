Source last updated 5 June 2026. Checked for new figures 25 November 2026.

About this data

Both sources count patients who have been admitted to hospital but are still waiting in an emergency department for a bed, at 8am. The CSO publishes the HSE's daily count some months later, so the site saves the HSE's own figure each morning to keep the record up to date.

The CSO's figures run to 31 December 2025. From November 2026 the site saves the HSE's count each morning, and those readings will appear here after the CSO's figures.

The HSE page gives a weekday but no date, so a saved reading is used only when that weekday matches the day it was saved. Readings are not checked against the CSO's figures until the CSO publishes them.

The Irish Nurses and Midwives Organisation publishes its own daily count, which is usually higher because it also includes patients on trolleys on wards.

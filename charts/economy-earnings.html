Average weekly earnings

Average weekly earnings of employees, in cash terms and adjusted for consumer prices (in 2026 Q2 prices), 2008 Q1 to 2026 Q2.

Average weekly earnings

Data from Central Statistics Office (CSO), Average Earnings, Hours Worked, Employment and Labour Costs (EHQ03) (CC BY 4.0); Central Statistics Office (CSO), Consumer Price Index (CPM20) (CC BY 4.0).

About this data

Earnings are gross, before tax, and include irregular payments such as bonuses. They cover all employees, full-time and part-time, so changes in the mix of jobs and hours also move the average.

The price-adjusted line uses the Consumer Price Index averaged over each quarter, so every figure is shown in 2026 Q2 prices.

Figures are not seasonally adjusted, so some of the movement from quarter to quarter is seasonal.

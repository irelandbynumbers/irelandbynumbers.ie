Source last updated 28 October 2026. Checked for new figures 14 November 2026.

About this data

The CSO measures the distance along the road network from each home to the nearest service, weighted by the number of people living there. Half of people in each county live closer than the median distance and half live further away.

The distance is to the nearest GP practice, which may not be taking new patients.

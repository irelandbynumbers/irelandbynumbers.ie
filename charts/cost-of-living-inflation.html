Data from Central Statistics Office (CSO), Consumer Price Index (CPM20) (CC BY 4.0).

About this data

The Consumer Price Index measures the change in the average price of the goods and services that households buy, weighted by how much households spend on each.

A negative figure means prices were lower on average than a year earlier.

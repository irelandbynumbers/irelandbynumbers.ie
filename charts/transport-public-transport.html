About this data

Dublin city buses are the services the CSO labels Dublin Metro bus. Other buses are the remaining bus services in the National Transport Authority's figures. Rail includes the DART. Rail figures start in 2020. Bus and rail figures come from the National Transport Authority, and Luas figures from Transport Infrastructure Ireland. Recent weeks may include estimates.

Each point is the average of four weeks, to smooth out holidays. The data file has the weekly figures.

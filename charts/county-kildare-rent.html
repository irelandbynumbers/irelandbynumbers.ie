Average rent in County Kildare

Average monthly rent on new private tenancies registered with the Residential Tenancies Board, 2008 to 2025.

Average rent in County Kildare

Data from Central Statistics Office (CSO), RTB Average Monthly Rent Report (RIA02) (CC BY 4.0).

Source last updated 14 May 2026. Checked for new figures 13 November 2026.

About this data

Rents are those agreed on new tenancies registered with the RTB in each year, for all property types and sizes. They are not adjusted for inflation.

Rents on existing tenancies, and social housing rents, are not included.
